# "It's just the nervous system"

Section: Part 3: Practice: What you need to do to actually get better: Main practice: respond well to symptoms
Issa's entry: "It's just the nervous system" (CFS Recovery/Miguel)
YouTube title: My go to mantra whenever my symptoms flared up #cfsrecovery #cfsme #anxiety #somaticsymptoms. CFS Recovery, 2023-02-06.

When I used to get random symptoms all the time, here is one mantra that I used to tell myself to help calm me down. I used to remind myself hundreds of times per day, it's just my nervous system. And the reason that really worked is because it brought with it a lot of logic. It made recovery much more logical than emotional.

Instead of just crossing my fingers and relying on motivation and hyping myself up, telling myself that, hey, you're actually going to survive, you're not gonna die, I was able to look at my symptoms from an objective point of view and really put a label on it, telling myself, hey, this is just a glitch in the nervous system. This is just my nervous system.

So every time I felt weird symptoms, a heart palpitation didn't mean I was having a heart attack. A weird headache didn't mean that I was having a stroke. When I would get a lot of shortness of breath, it didn't mean that my lungs were shutting down. All it was was a miscommunication of how my brain was sending signals to my body.

So the next time you find yourself really worrying about symptoms, try just reminding yourself it's just a nervous system.
